Delete your MindMate account

This page lets you request permanent deletion of your MindMate account and all of your stored data — even if you no longer have the app installed.

Important: account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Every record tied to your account is removed. There is no restore or undo.

Which app and developer is this for?

This is the official deletion page for MindMate, built by the Junior Achievers team of FG Enugu (JA FGCE) — the action-first mental-wellness companion for Android.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following is removed:

  • Mood check-ins and their impact notes
  • Journal entries
  • CBT thought records
  • Daily snapshot (wellness) check-ins
  • Professional appointment requests and their history
  • Trusted contacts and support events
  • Meditation and breathing session history
  • Your account profile and sign-in (you will be signed out)

Your app settings (like theme) are stored on your device and are cleared if you uninstall the app.

Option 1 — delete from the app (fastest)

If you still have MindMate installed on your phone:

  1. Open Settings → Privacy and data → Delete account.
  2. Follow the confirmation steps and re-enter your password.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Your data is removed and you are signed out.

Option 2 — request deletion from this page (no app needed)

If you uninstalled the app or lost access, submit the request form below. Include the email address you registered with and, if you remember it, your phone number — that's how we identify your account.

This is a competition prototype, so requests are handled directly by the Junior Achievers team. Allow up to a few days for processing; we'll confirm by email once your account and data are gone.

Submit a deletion request Email the team directly

If neither option above is available to you, contact the team through the competition contact channels and ask for “MindMate account deletion”.

Questions about deletion?

Write to the support email (shown above once the request form is live) with “Account deletion question” in the subject line, or ask through the competition contact channels. We reply as quickly as we can.
