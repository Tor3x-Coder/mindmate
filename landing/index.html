How it works

One loop, designed around a hard moment.

MindMate is not a mood tracker that stops at a graph. Every session moves you from feeling stuck to doing one small thing — and learning from what happened.

  1. Check in Tell MindMate how you're feeling and what's weighing on you, in your own words.
  2. Understand the need It listens for the actual need behind the mood — not a label or a diagnosis.
  3. One small action You get one personalised, doable step: a guided meditation, a breathing pattern, a CBT exercise, or a chat.
  4. Try it Guided audio, captions, and gentle pacing walk you through the whole thing.
  5. Honest feedback Tell us if it helped, did nothing, or made things worse — there is no forced positivity.
  6. Next step or support Another small action, or clear routes to trusted contacts, emergency resources, or a professional.

Safety & privacy

Honest about what MindMate is — and isn't.

Not a therapist, doctor, or diagnosis tool

MindMate supports self-help and reflection. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care. If you're in crisis, use the in-app emergency resources or contact local emergency services directly.

Transparent AI

The companion is clearly an AI. It is kept away from safety decisions: crisis content is routed to human support resources immediately, before any AI response.

Your data stays yours

Your records are stored under your own account with owner-only access rules. You can permanently delete everything from the app's Settings — or request deletion from this website, no reinstall needed.

No silent contacts

MindMate never calls or messages anyone on your behalf. Trusted-contact actions only happen when you explicitly tap them.

Emergency support is in-app and honest

Nigeria state numbers, the nationwide 112 fallback, international lines, and a helpline directory. For immediate danger, always call your local emergency number directly.

A prototype, proudly

This is a competition prototype: free, small, and focused on one excellent journey rather than fake depth.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is MindMate a therapy app?

No. It's an AI-supported self-help companion. It can't diagnose or treat anything, and it always points to real human support — trusted contacts, emergency resources, and professionals — when the moment needs it.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you record (mood check-ins, journal, thought records, appointments, wellness snapshots) is stored under your account with owner-only access. You can delete all of it permanently from the app or from the delete-account page on this site.

Does it work offline?

Guided audio, captions, and the app interface work offline. Saving your records and the AI companion need a network connection.

What if I'm in a crisis or emergency?

Use the Emergency Support screen in the app: Nigeria state lines with the nationwide 112 fallback, international numbers, and a helpline directory. MindMate is not an emergency service — for immediate danger, call your local emergency number directly.

Is it free?

Yes — this is a competition prototype, free to try.
